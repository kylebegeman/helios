Evidence. Fresh bootstrap installs its pinned candidate before checking capabilities/version. Failure stops before host setup but leaves that candidate installed. Differing existing installations already require upgrade.

Experience and tradeoffs. Verify a temporary executable, then publish. Needs a staging, cancellation, cleanup and retry lifecycle while preserving checksum, machine and state binding. Highest-return setup hardening.

Evidence. Ordinary rehearse already hashes, copies and verifies source; named-target moves upload snapshots. Local move stage and move test still build from supplied paths.

Experience and tradeoffs. Extend the capture contract to these workflows, with operation-owned retention and upload binding. Costs disk and copying; unlocks shared build provenance and reproducible retries.

APP-RESTORE-PROGRESSDurable ordinary-restore retriesLarge complexity · High impactExplain and safely resume an interrupted restore.

Evidence. Ordinary restore already pins its snapshot and resumes completed journal steps. Replaying an unfinished step after it wrote data, or starting another restore against partial data, can meet existing-data guards.

Experience and tradeoffs. Record operation-owned per-volume progress and distinguish partial restore data from pre-existing user data. Requires compatibility and marker design plus PostgreSQL/storage acceptance. A strong long-term reliability investment.

GD-O04Durable webhook delivery identityMedium complexity · High impactReturn the existing attempt when a delivery is repeated.

Experience and tradeoffs. Link authenticated delivery identities to existing deployment receipts, preserving push coalescing and explicit retry after failure. Requires schema authorization and retry policy. Unlocks delivery diagnostics across restart.
